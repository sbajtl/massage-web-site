Away from the crowds

Venera is a small massage studio in Galižana, just 4 km from Pula, surrounded by greenery and away from the bustle of town. Locals visit all year round, and in summer guests from the coast join them.

We're easy to reach, with free parking right outside the entrance: no hunting for a space and no rush. You step straight from your car into a calm, comfortable room.

Every massage is adjusted to you: the pressure, the pace and the areas you want us to focus on. We work with quality oils and creams, in a quiet room with soft light.
